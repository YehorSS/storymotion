Case — Ferrylink
Case study · brand + app · 2026

Ferries that feel like trains.

ClientFerrylink, 14 routes
ScopeBrand, app, signage
Time7 months
Result+38 % app bookings
01
Problem

Nobody knew which boat was theirs.

Four logos, three apps, tickets on paper. Queues started an hour before departure.

02 · Research

Two weeks on the decks.

We rode every route and timed every step from car park to seat.

03
Idea

Routes as lines, ports as stations.

A metro map for the sea: one colour per route, used on boats, signs and the app.

04 · Product

One tap to the right gate.

The ticket becomes a live card: gate, delay, deck, all in the route colour.

05
Launch

Repainted 22 boats in one winter.

Signs went up on all 14 routes the same morning. Queues halved in the first month.

more trips booked in the app, and half the queue at the gate.
